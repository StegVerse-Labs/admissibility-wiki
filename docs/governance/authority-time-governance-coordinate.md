# Authority × Time Governance Coordinate

StegVerse governance is located by two coordinates:

```text
G = (Authority, Time)
```

Authority answers under whose governing relation or basis a transition is evaluated. Time answers at what governing moment or interval that authority applies.

State, identity, policy, delegation, consent, evidence, verification, context, boundary, recoverability, capability, uncertainty, and continuity are evaluated at that coordinate. They are not additional governance coordinates.

## Temporal non-causality

Time being a governance coordinate does not mean elapsed time independently creates governance change.

```text
Delta-time -/-> Delta-authority
```

Wall-clock readings, timestamps, heartbeat cadence, freshness, expiry, and duration may be evidence or policy inputs. They are not Authority. Time itself remains the coordinate at which the applicable Authority and surrounding conditions are evaluated.

## Commit-time authority

Commit-time authority is therefore naturally expressed as Authority resolved at the Time at which consequence would bind. A prior approval may remain historically valid while no longer being applicable at a later governance coordinate because state, policy, delegation, evidence, or another relevant condition changed.

## Verification

Verification provides evidence about a system, claim, event, or transition. It does not create a governance coordinate and does not manufacture Authority. Verification may affect admissibility only insofar as the applicable governance rules at `(Authority, Time)` consume that evidence.

## Transition geometry

Relational Transition Geometry describes what is changing and the relationships relevant to the transition. Governance evaluates that transition geometry at `(Authority, Time)`; RTG does not replace the governance coordinate system.
