## Why this record matters

The most consequential result of the underlying conversations is not merely that TA-14 reviewed StegVerse. After repeatedly directing StegVerse toward TA-14's own product and Exchange procedure, TA-14 voluntarily created a public review artifact that can itself be tested against the independence, standing, authority, neutrality, attribution, preservation, reproducibility, and route-completeness requirements it applies to others.

StegVerse invited independent, neutral, publicly reviewable testing from the beginning and repeated that invitation. The preserved review therefore provides a durable comparison between:

1. the standard TA-14 demanded from StegVerse;
2. the method TA-14 used to evaluate StegVerse;
3. the authority TA-14 attributes to its resulting determination; and
4. the reciprocal evidentiary questions StegVerse raised about TA-14.

This is not treated as proof of bad faith. It is treated as a highly useful public test object.

## Accepted findings

TA-14 produced a valuable public-evidence gap analysis whose technical requirements materially overlap with StegVerse's work in:

- commit-time admissibility;
- continuity and omission resistance;
- authority establishment and revalidation;
- exact-object binding;
- atomic commit and TOCTOU closure;
- non-bypassable execution restraint;
- trusted receipt issuance and signer provenance;
- independent replay;
- external outcome correspondence; and
- proof of restraint or verified non-occurrence.

StegVerse accepts the value of publishing one canonical, independently reproducible, route-complete evidence package addressing these concerns.

The strongest technical findings concern:

- evaluated-object versus committed-object identity;
- parameter closure and anti-substitution evidence;
- reservation, lease, expiry, cancellation, and compare-and-commit behavior;
- alternate execution paths and direct-runtime bypass;
- signer trust, rotation, and revocation;
- expected sequence, checkpoints, omission detection, and closure;
- system-of-record or environmental outcome evidence; and
- independent reconstruction from pinned artifacts.

These are appropriate remediation targets whether or not every characterization in the review is accepted.

## Findings not accepted as established

### Exchange exclusivity

StegVerse does not accept that external-review validity depends upon submission through a process controlled by the institution being reviewed.

TA-14's Exchange may be one review route. Its exclusivity, independence, authority, neutrality, and standing are separate propositions requiring attributable, preserved, reproducible, and independently reviewable evidence.

The absence of a TA-14 route identifier or Exchange submission establishes that TA-14 did not receive a bounded Exchange package. It does not establish that TA-14 controls the only valid method of external examination.

### Independent adjudication

The review is prepared by TA-14, evaluates criticism of TA-14, applies TA-14's own chain, defends TA-14's preferred procedure, and concludes with a TA-14 determination. It is therefore a self-authored institutional review, not an independently issued adjudication.

The reciprocal classification is:

```text
StegVerse Wiki finding: self-authored public analysis pending independent reproduction
TA-14 determination: self-authored public review pending independent reproduction
```

Publication can preserve evidence and make a method reproducible. Publication alone does not create neutral reviewer standing.

### Self-authorship versus reproducibility

The review is correct that a self-authored wiki does not become independent merely because it is public. But independent authorship and independent reproducibility are different properties.

A self-authored finding may still provide:

- exact source references;
- preserved test fixtures;
- executable methods;
- expected and observed results;
- bounded conclusions; and
- an open challenge procedure.

Those properties permit independent reproduction even when the initial author is not independent.

### Fallacy findings

Several claimed fallacies in the TA-14 document are not paired with complete immutable quotations, context, formal interpretation rules, competing interpretations, and reproducible adjudication records. They are therefore retained as TA-14's characterizations of the exchange, not as independently verified findings.

## Reciprocal evidence rule

The same standard applies in every direction:

```text
claim -> attributable source
source -> preserved object
object -> integrity binding
method -> executable or reconstructable procedure
procedure -> bounded inputs and environment
result -> expected versus observed record
finding -> scoped conclusion
standing -> independently established authority
challenge -> open reproducible route
```

No framework receives independence, authority, neutrality, or universal validity merely by naming its own process.

## Activation target

The principal remediation target is one public reference route:

```text
authoritative input
-> independent authority resolution
-> evidence sufficiency determination
-> admissibility decision
-> exact-object binding
-> atomic commit or refusal
-> effector-side execution or rejection evidence
-> external outcome or protected-state evidence
-> ordered closure receipts
-> independent replay
```

The route must include adversarial cases for stale authority, object substitution, direct-runtime bypass, missing receipts, signer revocation, receipt-service outage, mutation, replay, alternate execution paths, and DENY with verified non-occurrence.

## Bounded determination

TA-14's review is technically valuable, rhetorically adversarial, and procedurally self-interested. Its substantive gap analysis is accepted as remediation input. Its claimed or implied power to define the exclusive valid review route is not accepted absent reciprocal independent proof.

This page does not claim that TA-14 is invalid, that StegVerse has already closed the identified gaps, or that either party's self-authored publication constitutes neutral adjudication.
