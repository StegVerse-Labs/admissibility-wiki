# StegVerse Formalism Declaration

**Status:** Proposed architecture; not presented as established external science.
**Goal ID:** `SRC-PHASE3-001`

The following constructs are StegVerse-specific formalisms assembled from, but not equivalent to, concepts in signal processing, formal methods, distributed systems, trust infrastructure, AI governance, and evidence management.

## 1. Standing-Signal Layer

A governance abstraction applied after signal acquisition and representation. It evaluates whether a signal or derived claim may become load-bearing for a governed transition.

It is not a third physical encoding regime after analog and digital.

## 2. Transition Element

The canonical minimal governed action record within StegVerse. A transition element binds the proposed change, actor or source, temporal coordinate, policy references, evidence references, authority state, admissibility result, and reconstruction references.

It is not claimed to be a universal minimal unit across science or computing.

## 3. Commit-Time Admissibility Reconstruction

A decision procedure that evaluates whether authority, delegation, policy, evidence freshness, destination conditions, and required continuity guarantees are valid at the moment a transition would affect reality.

Historical validity and replayability do not automatically satisfy this check.

## 4. Namespaced Standing Classes

StegVerse separates at least:

- evidentiary standing;
- interpretive standing;
- identity standing;
- authority standing;
- delegation standing;
- policy standing;
- transition standing;
- current admissibility.

No single score may silently substitute for these independent determinations.

## 5. Dead Basis

An artifact, signal, record, policy, delegation, or evidence object that remains historically present but lacks a live and valid path required to support a current governed decision.

Historical preservation does not make a dead basis load-bearing.

## 6. Standing Correction

A fail-closed governance action that prevents a structurally intact artifact or signal from contributing to a transition when its current evidence, authority, policy, or interpretation standing fails.

Standing correction does not erase history; it changes current load-bearing status.

## 7. Bounded Micro-Node

A scoped human, software, or AI work entity with explicit capabilities, revocable authority, collision boundaries, and receipt obligations. A micro-node may execute authorized work but cannot create its own sovereignty, enlarge its mandate, or convert successful operation into governing authority.

## 8. Recoverability Under Degrading Authority

A boundary-admissibility requirement that asks whether a governed state remains inspectable, contestable, and recoverable when an operator, agent, credential, or governing actor loses coherence, availability, or authority.

This is a proposed StegVerse requirement, not an established theorem of control theory, physiology, or distributed computing.

## 9. Reconstructable Decision Standing

The ability to independently reproduce and inspect the evidence, ordering, authority, delegation, policy, and decision conditions that produced an admissibility result.

This term is preferred over `reconstructable legitimacy` unless substantive legal, ethical, or institutional legitimacy has been independently assessed.

## Formalism labeling rule

Normative doctrine and public projections must identify these constructs with language such as:

```text
Proposed StegVerse formalism
StegVerse-defined governance construct
Architectural extension informed by established fields
```

They must not use language implying that the construct is already an accepted scientific law, universal taxonomy, clinical diagnostic method, or industry standard.

## Validation obligation

Each formalism requires:

1. a machine-readable definition;
2. explicit inputs and outputs;
3. declared assumptions;
4. failure and UNKNOWN behavior;
5. deterministic test fixtures where applicable;
6. a claims-register entry;
7. a doctrine-revision-matrix entry;
8. evidence that implementation does not exceed the stated authority boundary.
