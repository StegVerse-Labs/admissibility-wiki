## Adoption rule

A doctrine claim may move from this register into normative doctrine only when:

1. its determination is not `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`, `CONTRADICTED`, or `UNKNOWN`;
2. its source class and scope are explicit;
3. analogies are not presented as equivalences;
4. a StegVerse-specific extension is labeled as proposed;
5. validation requirements and authority boundaries are stated;
6. the doctrine revision matrix records the exact change.

## Known corpus limitation

The supplied Group B and Group H artifacts are research scaffolds, not complete literature reviews. Claims relying materially on control theory, dynamical systems, neuroscience, BCI, bioinformatics, or computational biology remain provisional until substantive reviews are installed.
