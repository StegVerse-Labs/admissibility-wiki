## Fail-closed rule

An open question cannot be silently converted into a doctrine assertion. It must remain labeled `UNKNOWN`, `PROPOSED`, `RESERVED`, `BLOCKED`, or `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` until its release condition is satisfied.
