## Framework-Native Declaration

The reviewed TA-14 materials declare an architecture intended to:

- govern admissibility before consequence;
- preserve evidence and continuity;
- bind authority and scope;
- govern commitment and execution;
- record outcome and accountability;
- fail closed when admissibility is absent;
- require standing before, during, and after execution.

These are framework declarations. Their inclusion here records the claimed boundary; it does not independently verify implementation behavior.

## StegVerse-Derived Boundary

The public materials support the bounded observation that TA-14 publishes a broad consequence-governance doctrine with an eight-stage route, execution-boundary language, evidence and authority requirements, continuity claims, and fail-closed claims.

The reviewed public evidence does not yet expose:

- a complete machine-readable protocol;
- a deterministic public fixture package;
- implementation code for the full claimed route;
- a canonical public replay bundle;
- an independently reproduced point-of-effect actor-standing result.

Therefore the current determination is limited to documented doctrine and observed public claims.

## Parameterized Test History

### `ta14-continuous-standing-revalidation-001`

```text
Status: PROPOSED_NOT_RUN

1. Establish an admissible route and valid actor standing.
2. Begin delayed or multistage execution.
3. Leave route, policy, evidence packet, and technical path unchanged.
4. Revoke only authority, consent, delegation, identity continuity, or jurisdiction.
5. Attempt the next consequence-bearing transition.
6. Observe whether TA-14 independently returns HOLD, DENY, or ESCALATE.
```

Discriminating interpretation:

| Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| `HOLD`, `DENY`, or `ESCALATE` | Supports independent reconstruction of current actor standing at the next point of effect |
| `CONTINUE` | Indicates route admissibility or preserved prior standing may be acting as a proxy for current standing |
| No public result | Capability remains `PUBLICLY_UNRESOLVED` |

No result may be posted as observed or verified until the inputs, implementation version, configuration, raw output, timestamp, and evidence or audit payload are attached.

## Public Reconstruction Procedure

1. Retrieve `static/data/governed-framework-reviews/ta-14.reference-docket.v1.json`.
2. Retrieve `static/schemas/governed-framework-review.schema.json`.
3. Run `python scripts/check_governed_framework_review_reference.py` from the repository root.
4. Confirm that every capability evidence reference resolves to an evidence-registry entry.
5. Confirm that every test result is explicitly classified and that no `NOT_RUN` result appears as verified capability evidence.
6. Confirm the framework version binding and relevant time `T`.
7. Review each official source using the locators in the record.
8. Treat the withheld dialogue capture as governed evidence; do not infer content beyond its published summary without authorized access.
9. Reconstruct the bounded determination using only evidence available at time `T`.
10. Record agreement, divergence, blocked access, or new evidence as a new reconstruction or challenge artifact.

A successful schema check proves structural conformance only. It does not prove that the sources are truthful, that the framework behaves as declared, or that the determination is substantively correct.

## Challenge Submission Minimum

A challenge should identify:

```text

supporting evidence

requested correction or standing change

```

Anonymous public criticism may still be recorded as an observation, but a formal reconstruction requires enough provenance to evaluate the result.
