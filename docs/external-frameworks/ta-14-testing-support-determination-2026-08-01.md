# TA-14 Testing Support Determination — Public Demonstrations

## Determination

```text
Framework: TA-14
Date: 2026-08-01
Decision: FURTHER PUBLIC-DEMO TESTING AND EVALUATION NOT SUPPORTED
Scope: TA-14 public demonstrations, authenticated demonstration workspaces,
       and the owner-controlled testing arrangement observed by StegVerse
Paid evaluation capability: NOT ADJUDICATED
Underlying private architecture: NOT ADJUDICATED
Authority granted: none
```

StegVerse will not continue testing the available TA-14 demonstration artifacts under the presently offered arrangement.

This decision is not based on an inference that the paid environment lacks additional modules. TA-14's own pages state that demonstration pages may not use every module available in the paid review. That statement is part of the reason continued demo testing is not supported.

## Decisive structural concern

The observed disclosure and testing structure is:

```text
broad public governance rhetoric
-> partial public demonstrations
-> account creation required for meaningful access
-> strongest capabilities not required to appear in the demonstrations
-> missing capability may be attributed to modules used only in the paid review
-> fuller verification remains inside an owner-controlled commercial path
```

This structure prevents the public demonstrations from resolving the broader claims that create interest, authority, and commercial demand.

The issue is not:

```text
public demo limitation
-> proof that the paid system lacks the capability
```

The issue is:

```text
public rhetoric receives the benefit of the broader capability claim
while the public artifact is expressly permitted not to demonstrate it.

If the demo appears persuasive:
-> it supports the rhetoric.

If the demo does not demonstrate the rhetoric:
-> the missing capability may be assigned to the paid environment.
```

That is an asymmetrical evidentiary posture. The strongest public claims cannot be decisively tested through the public artifacts, while the path said to contain the fuller capability is controlled and monetized by the claimant.

## Revenue-funnel effect

StegVerse records the resulting structure as a commercial funnel **in effect**:

```text
public claims generate attention and perceived governance standing
-> partial demonstrations create apparent public accessibility
-> meaningful capability correspondence remains unresolved
-> fuller review requires entry into a paid, owner-controlled process
```

This determination does not claim access to the owner's private motive. It evaluates the observable structure and effect of the offered process.

A governance claimant cannot receive durable public standing from claims whose meaningful verification is both controlled and monetized by that claimant.

## Account-gated transparency concern

Meaningful access to the reviewed demonstrations required account creation and sign-in.

The tester did not establish, before supplying account-linked information, an explicit and independently verifiable boundary covering:

```text
data minimization
retention duration
deletion procedure
secondary use
owner access
analytics or profiling
non-reuse for commercial or model-development purposes
```

This record does not claim unlawful retention or misuse. It records that continued testing would require additional account-linked interaction without a sufficiently established privacy and retention boundary.

## Neutral public scrutiny concern

The observed arrangement did not provide a frozen, neutral, reciprocal, publicly reconstructable test in which:

```text
inputs are fixed in advance
rules are inspectable
outputs are preserved
receipts are independently retrievable
disagreement is preserved
both frameworks may evaluate each other
public observers may reconstruct the result
```

Reciprocal architectural testing was declined under the proposed model, while stronger capability remained associated with a separate paid evaluation path.

Under StegVerse doctrine, governance must remain publicly scrutinizable, reconstructable, and falsifiable. A process that controls the only meaningful route to testing its strongest claims does not satisfy that requirement.

## Why further testing is not supported

```text
1. Additional demo testing would produce more bounded observations about artifacts
   that are expressly allowed not to exercise the full paid-review module set.

2. A failed or incomplete demo observation cannot resolve the broader claim because
   the missing function may always be attributed to an undisclosed paid module.

3. A successful demo observation still does not establish the broader system unless
   claim-to-artifact correspondence, evidence derivation, and independent replay are exposed.

4. Continued account use adds tester labor and account-linked exposure without creating
   a path to decisive public verification.

5. The offered structure subordinates transparent governance scrutiny to a commercial,
   claimant-controlled access boundary.
```

Therefore:

> Further TA-14 public-demo testing and evaluation is not supported. The demonstrations are structurally incapable of resolving the broader public rhetoric because they are partial by declaration, account-gated in operation, and separated from the fuller paid environment said to contain additional capability. The resulting arrangement functions as a commercial verification funnel and conflicts with StegVerse's requirement that governance claims be openly reconstructable, falsifiable, and independently scrutinizable.

## Conditions that would support reopening

Testing may be reconsidered only if a materially different evaluation path is offered, such as:

```text
no-account or clearly bounded pseudonymous access
published retention, deletion, and non-reuse terms before data submission
a frozen neutral test package
publicly inspectable input and rule manifests
complete claim-to-module mapping
machine-readable outputs and receipts
independently retrievable preservation
public replay and reconstruction
reciprocal evaluation
preserved disputes and corrections
no requirement to purchase access to verify the public claims under review
```

A paid service may exist. The governance objection arises when payment controls the only meaningful path for evaluating claims already used to establish public governance credibility.

## Authority boundary

```text
public-demo limitation != proof of paid-system incapability
additional paid modules != public substantiation of public rhetoric
commercial service != misconduct by itself
observable commercial-funnel effect != proof of private motive
account requirement != proof of unlawful retention
halt of StegVerse testing != universal prohibition on independent review
public claim != public standing
visibility != verification
marketing accessibility != neutral governance scrutiny
```
