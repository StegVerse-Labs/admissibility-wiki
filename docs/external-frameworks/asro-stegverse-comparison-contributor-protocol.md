# ASRO–StegVerse Comparison Contributor Protocol

## Status

```text
protocol_status: STEGVERSE_PROPOSED_COMPARISON_SPECIFIC_PROTOCOL
bilateral_authorization: false
partnership_created: false
shared_ownership_created: false
future_work_guaranteed: false
execution_authority_created: false
```

This protocol is a narrow StegVerse proposal for governing contributions, corrections, attribution, reuse, and publication within the bounded ASRO–StegVerse comparison. It is not a general collaboration agreement and becomes bilateral only after both owners approve its exact language.

## Scope

The protocol applies only to:

- source messages and public materials intentionally introduced into the bounded comparison;
- owner declarations;
- StegVerse-generated derivatives and test artifacts;
- ASRO-side evidence expressly approved for the selected public test surface;
- correspondence, correction, replay, reconstruction, and comparison records arising from that bounded work.

It does not govern either party's unrelated repositories, private materials, internal methods, future products, trademarks, or independent analysis.

## Contribution threshold

A contribution is ledger-eligible when it materially affects one or more of:

- terminology or declared meaning;
- artifact structure;
- provenance classification;
- test design or expected result;
- authority or non-authority boundary;
- publication scope;
- correction, replay, reconstruction, or receipt integrity.

Routine acknowledgements, scheduling messages, and unchanged repetition need not be recorded.

## Attribution

Each recorded contribution should identify, when available:

```text
date
contributor
source message, public source, or commit
contribution type
originating framework or owner
resulting derivative or decision
attribution language
authorization state
later correction or supersession
```

Attribution records contribution and provenance. It does not create ownership of another party's independently produced artifact, partnership, endorsement, certification, or authority.

## Ownership and control

- Each owner controls its own source declaration and independently produced artifacts.
- StegVerse controls StegVerse-generated derivatives, transition receipts, and admissibility results.
- ASRO retains control of ASRO-native materials and evidence.
- A reviewer-side profile or seam record may not alter either controlling source declaration.
- A bilateral record is separately controlled by its exact mutually authorized language; it does not merge the underlying systems or ownership domains.

## Reuse rights

Public material may be cited and analyzed within applicable public-source and license boundaries with accurate provenance and non-endorsement language.

Private, internal, unpublished, or unreleased material may not be incorporated, reconstructed, published, or treated as an owner source without separate explicit authorization.

This proposed protocol does not grant either party a general license to the other's marks, confidential information, software, schemas, or future work.

## Correction and dispute process

1. Identify the challenged claim, artifact, field, or attribution.
2. State the reason and supporting evidence.
3. Record the challenge in an append-only ledger or correction record.
4. Apply corrections through additive commits or visibly superseding records.
5. Recompute dependent hashes, manifests, runs, and receipts when their inputs change.
6. Preserve unresolved disagreement rather than silently collapsing it.
7. Do not publish a bilateral correction as mutually authorized until both owners approve the exact language.

## Future opportunities

Future work is possible but not guaranteed. Participation in this comparison creates no obligation to provide:

- later testing;
- ASRO-native execution;
- private runtime configuration;
- independent review;
- commercial opportunity;
- integration, adoption, or continued collaboration.

## Contributor protection

No contributor should be represented as a partner, member, participant in an integrated system, certifying party, endorser, or authority source unless that exact role is separately and explicitly accepted.

Source contributions and derived artifacts must remain visibly separated. Corrections must preserve prior public history and accurately credit both the source provider and derivative creator.

## Observation versus authority

Observation, correspondence, replay, reconstruction, review, and contribution do not themselves grant execution authority, admissibility, certification, custody, or authority inheritance.

## Publication control

- Unilateral owner analysis must be labeled unilateral.
- Any bilateral Seam Comparison Record requires exact-language authorization from both owners.
- Any main-site ASRO projection beyond public unilateral analysis requires the applicable publication authorization.
- A bilateral record must be labeled bounded, owner-authorized, and not independently reviewed unless independent review has actually occurred.

## ASRO attribution placeholder

Where mutually approved public material identifies ASRO, the comparison should use the exact attribution approved by the ASRO owner, including the appropriate trademark symbol, responsible developer/operator, and pinned public repository evidence. This protocol does not itself validate trademark status or authorize text not yet approved by both sides.

## Non-authority boundary

This proposed protocol creates no partnership, agency, joint venture, joint ownership, certification, endorsement, integration, execution authority, admissibility, custody transfer, or downstream mutation authority.
