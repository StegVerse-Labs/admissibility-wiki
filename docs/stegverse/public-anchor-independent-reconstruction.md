## Minimum review scope

An accountable reviewer should:

1. inspect the frozen doctrine, schemas, docket records, status records, validators, and routes;
2. distinguish structural validation from substantive correctness;
3. reconstruct TA-14, ASRO, and StegVerse self-review standing from cited evidence, tests, uncertainty, and challenge state;
4. evaluate whether the self-review adequately discloses conflicts of role and authority;
5. preserve every divergence rather than overwriting the source records.

## Required submission contents

A reconstruction submission should include:

```text
reviewer identity and reviewer type
declared conflicts
independence basis
method description
artifact references
reconstructed result
difference summary
missing evidence
stable public artifact or signed receipt when available
```

Permitted results are:

```text
REPRODUCED
PARTIAL
DIVERGENT
BLOCKED
```

A divergent or blocked result is valid review evidence. The system must not force agreement merely to produce convergence.

## Reviewer accountability boundary

```text
anonymous result != standing change
invitation != reviewer appointment
claimed independence != established independence
submission != automatic determination
reconstruction != certification
reconstruction != execution authority
publication != government recognition
```

The reviewer must disclose identity, reviewer type, conflicts, method, artifact references, and the basis for any independence claim.

An anonymous or unattributed observation may still be preserved as public input, but it cannot automatically change docket standing.

```text
freeze target
  -> identify accountable reviewer
  -> disclose conflicts and method
  -> inspect evidence without accepting StegVerse determinations as authoritative
  -> reconstruct each docket
  -> record agreement and divergence
  -> validate submission structure
  -> enter submission as evidence
  -> conduct governed standing review
  -> preserve correction and supersession history
```

## Standing effect

Receipt of a structurally valid reconstruction does not automatically change standing.

Any standing change requires a separate governed determination that evaluates:

- reviewer accountability;
- independence basis;
- conflicts of interest;
- method adequacy;
- artifact identity;
- relevant time T;
- completeness;
- divergence;
- challenge and correction history.

## Public challenge

Any person, organization, framework, AI entity, public body, or research group may inspect the frozen target and submit a reconstruction. Participation does not require agreement with StegVerse vocabulary or determinations.

The purpose is not ontology capture. The purpose is public, reconstructable disagreement with evidence preserved.
